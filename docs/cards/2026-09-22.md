# Cards — 22 September 2026

Victoria released its data centre plan today. Status on every card; the note is
what must travel with it. See [`README.md`](README.md) for what the statuses
mean. **Post nothing above its status.**

---

## 01 · CLAIM — the rules arrive

> Victoria will prohibit data centres **in residential zones**, ban them near
> schools and childcare, and require a 150-metre buffer from homes.

▸ PREMIER OF VICTORIA · "WE NEED DATA CENTRES BUT WE SET THE RULES"
▸ RELEASED 22·09·2026 · PREMIER.VIC.GOV.AU

*Highlight:* `in residential zones`
*Status:* **SOURCED** — verbatim from the media release.
*Note if used:* it is two mechanisms, not one. A prohibition in residential
*zones*, and separately a 150 m buffer between a data centre building and homes
where they are permitted. Quoted as only one, it will be corrected.

## 02 · CLAIM — no drinking water

> Victorian data centres will be required to cool with **recycled or
> non-drinking water** — or offset what they use and pay for the upgrades.

▸ PREMIER OF VICTORIA · DATA CENTRE PLAN
▸ RELEASED 22·09·2026

*Highlight:* `recycled or non-drinking water`
*Status:* **SOURCED**
*Note if used:* the government says under 1% of the state's drinking water
currently goes to data centre cooling, and concedes it should be zero. The
offset provision means the requirement is not absolute at commencement.

## 03 · CLAIM — grandfathered

> Victoria's new data centre rules **will not be retrospective**. Applications

▸ PREMIER OF VICTORIA · DATA CENTRE PLAN, 22·09·2026
▸ CIVIC INTERPLAY · DATACENTRES.CIVICINTERPLAY.IO

*Highlight:* `will not be retrospective`
*Status:* **SOURCED** for the exemption. **UNVERIFIED** for anything about how
many or which projects.
*Note if used — the numbers are not the government's:* the release gives no
count. ABC reports eight applications currently before the Minister. Attribute
that figure to ABC, 22 Sep 2026, never to Victoria.

## 04 · CLAIM — the cohort that escapes is the one already audited

> Every checkable Victorian data centre record this project audited discloses
> no energy or water figure. Those are the records the new rules exempt.

▸ CIVIC INTERPLAY · VICTORIAN DISCLOSURE AUDIT, 12 OF 12 RECORDS
▸ AGAINST VIC DATA CENTRE PLAN · 22·09·2026

*Highlight:* `no energy or water figure`
*Status:* **UNVERIFIED** as a joined claim — the audit is verified, the
exemption is sourced, the *connection between them* is this project's argument.
*Note if used:* say "12 of 12 checkable records" and note Westmeadows is
unknown because the exhibited documents were removed from the live site. Do not
round it to "every Victorian data centre".

## 05 · CLAIM — two states priced it in three weeks

> NSW proposed **$200,000 per megawatt** for network upgrades. Victoria now
> requires data centres to meet connection costs. Neither publishes who gets
> the capacity.

▸ NSW CONSULTATION 17·08·2026 · VIC DATA CENTRE PLAN 22·09·2026
▸ CIVIC INTERPLAY · DATACENTRES.CIVICINTERPLAY.IO

*Highlight:* `$200,000 per megawatt`
*Status:* **SOURCED** for both policies; **UNVERIFIED** for the joined argument.
*Note if used:* the $200k/MW is the NSW *default* rate for the
Sydney–Newcastle–Wollongong area, $100k/MW elsewhere, and it is a **proposal
under consultation**, not law. The point is the pairing: the price is becoming
public while the allocation stays in a connection agreement.
