# Cards — 19 September 2026

Headline claims from tonight's tracking, in the `sarah speaks` card format:
label chip · claim with one highlighted figure · two attribution lines.

**Nothing here is cleared for posting.** Each card carries a status. `VERIFIED`
means walked to the primary document by a person. `SOURCED` means the figure is
quoted correctly from a named document but nobody has independently checked it.
`UNVERIFIED` means it rests on this project's own computation and has not had an
adversarial pass. Post nothing above its status.

## 01 · CLAIM — the 66

> Data centre load accounts for **66 per cent** of the growth in NSW end-use
> energy consumption from FY26 to FY35.

▸ TRANSGRID · 2026 TRANSMISSION ANNUAL PLANNING REPORT, P.10
▸ PUBLISHED 2026 · READ 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `66 per cent`
*Status:* **SOURCED** — verbatim from the executive summary panel.
*Note if used:* it is 66% of growth in end-use consumption, not of consumption.
The decade it is measured against also contains electrifying transport, heating
and industry — everything else combined is the other third.

*Status:* **UNVERIFIED** — the comparison is this project's.

## 06 · CLAIM — capacity without consumption

> A 113-page engineering report for Australia's largest proposed data centre
> states its grid connection — **2,160 MVA** — and never once states how much
> electricity it will use.

▸ WESTERN DOWNS DIGITAL PARK · INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES REPORT, PP.101–103
▸ WESTERN DOWNS REGIONAL COUNCIL REGISTER · RETRIEVED 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `2,160 MVA`
*Status:* **SOURCED** for the figure; **UNVERIFIED** for the absence.
*Note if used:* no PUE, no WUE, no kWh, no MWh anywhere in 113 pages. A
connection size is how big the pipe is, not how much flows through it — and
with no efficiency figure stated, one cannot be derived from the other.

> 1,200 MW**, averaging 650 MW. Smaller ones connect to the distribution
> network instead.

▸ TRANSGRID · NSW INQUIRY SUBMISSION 114, 27·03·2026
▸ READ 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `250 MW to over 1,200 MW`
*Status:* **SOURCED**

to the network that plans and prices for households. Above it, a data centre

why Victoria's planner separately counts "small distribution connected data
centres".
