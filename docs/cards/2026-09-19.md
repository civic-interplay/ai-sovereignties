# Cards — 19 September 2026

Headline claims from tonight's tracking, in the `sarah speaks` card format:
label chip · claim with one highlighted figure · two attribution lines.

**Nothing here is cleared for posting.** Each card carries a status. `VERIFIED`
means walked to the primary document by a person. `SOURCED` means the figure is
quoted correctly from a named document but nobody has independently checked it.
`UNVERIFIED` means it rests on this project's own computation and has not had an
adversarial pass. Post nothing above its status.

## 01 · CLAIM — the 66

> Data centre load accounts for **66 per cent** of the growth in NSW end-use
> energy consumption from FY26 to FY35.

▸ TRANSGRID · 2026 TRANSMISSION ANNUAL PLANNING REPORT, P.10
▸ PUBLISHED 2026 · READ 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `66 per cent`
*Status:* **SOURCED** — verbatim from the executive summary panel.
*Note if used:* it is 66% of growth in end-use consumption, not of consumption.
The decade it is measured against also contains electrifying transport, heating
and industry — everything else combined is the other third.

## 03 · CLAIM — the queue

> NSW's grid operator has received data centre connection enquiries totalling
> **20 gigawatts** — roughly twice the entire state's peak demand.

▸ TRANSGRID · 2026 TAPR, P.20
▸ READ 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `20 gigawatts`
*Status:* **SOURCED**
*Note if used — important:* these are **enquiries, not commitments**. Transgrid
states plainly that some are speculative or duplicated across networks, which is
why Baringa was commissioned to de-duplicate. Never report this as approved or
committed capacity. This is the card most likely to be attacked, and the caveat
must travel with it.

## 05 · CLAIM — the two vocabularies

> One grid operator's annual report says "data centre" **187 times**. A decade
> of one council's planning applications says it **zero**.

▸ TRANSGRID 2026 TAPR (191PP) · CITY OF MORETON BAY OPEN DATA (16,519 RECORDS)
▸ BOTH READ 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `187 times`
*Status:* **UNVERIFIED** — the comparison is this project's.
*Note if used:* the honest framing is that these are different kinds of
document. The point is not that one is lazy; it is that the energy system has a
word for these facilities and the planning system does not. Queensland consents
them as "utility installation" or "high impact industry".

## 06 · CLAIM — capacity without consumption

> A 113-page engineering report for Australia's largest proposed data centre
> states its grid connection — **2,160 MVA** — and never once states how much
> electricity it will use.

▸ WESTERN DOWNS DIGITAL PARK · INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES REPORT, PP.101–103
▸ WESTERN DOWNS REGIONAL COUNCIL REGISTER · RETRIEVED 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `2,160 MVA`
*Status:* **SOURCED** for the figure; **UNVERIFIED** for the absence.
*Note if used:* no PUE, no WUE, no kWh, no MWh anywhere in 113 pages. A
connection size is how big the pipe is, not how much flows through it — and
with no efficiency figure stated, one cannot be derived from the other.

## 07 · CLAIM — the water that counts

> The same report discloses **16.5 kilolitres a day** of operating water. It
> discloses twenty times that for construction.

▸ WESTERN DOWNS DIGITAL PARK · INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES REPORT, PP.58–60, 107
▸ RETRIEVED 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `16.5 kilolitres a day`
*Status:* **SOURCED**
*Note if used:* construction demand is ~322 kL/day plus ~200 kL/day dust
suppression, and 26.9 ML one-off per building to commission the closed loop.
Both figures are real; the audit counts operating water only, and says so.

> Sydney is **$14.7 million**.

▸ NSW EPLANNING ONLINEDA · AWS SYD52, SMEATON GRANGE (PAN-139175)
▸ QUERIED 19·09·2026

*Highlight:* `$14.7 million`
*Status:* **SOURCED** — the figure is from the statutory feed.
*Note if used — important:* this is almost certainly *correct as the regulation
