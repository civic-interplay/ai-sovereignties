Conditions

1.     Pursuant to clause 27 of the Metropolitan Region Scheme, this approval is deemed
       to be an approval under clause 54(1) of the Metropolitan Region Scheme.

2.     This decision constitutes planning approval only and is valid for a period of four
       years from the date of approval. If the subject development is not substantially
       commenced within the specified period, the approval shall lapse and be of no
       further effect.

3.     Prior to applying for a Building Permit, the proponent shall submit to the City of
       Gosnells for approval a preliminary proposal for an art work designed by a
       professional artist at a cost of 1% of the total project cost (to a maximum of
       $250,000), to be located within the subject site as an integral part of the
       development. Alternatively, prior to occupation of the development, the
       owner/applicant shall make a cash contribution towards public art in lieu of the
       above.

       Prior to the commencement of development, and if not making a cash contribution,
       the proponent shall submit to the City of Gosnells for approval an 'Application for
       Artwork Design' and the proponent is to enter into a contract with a professional
       artist/s to design and install (if appropriate) the artwork approved by the City of
       Gosnells.

       Prior to the occupation of the development, the art work shall then be installed and
       maintained thereafter to the satisfaction of the City of Gosnells.

4.     Prior to applying for a Building Permit, an urban water management plan is to be
       prepared and approved, in consultation with the Department of Water and
       Environmental Regulation, consistent with any approved Local Water Management
       Strategy.

AMENDING MOTION 1

Moved by: Karen Hyde                                    Seconded by: Cr David Goode

That Condition No. 4 be amended to read as follows:

       Prior to applying for a Building Permit, an urban water management plan is to be
       prepared and approved in stages as required, in consultation with the Department
       of Water and Environmental Regulation, consistent with any approved Local Water
       Management Strategy.

The Amending Motion was put and CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

REASON: To allow sufficient flexibility to the condition to allow for a staged approval of
technical documentation and a staged building permit approval, whilst ensuring that each
stage of development is consistent with the staged urban water management plan

AMENDING MOTION 2

Moved by: Karen Hyde                                          Seconded by: Tony Arias

That a new Advice Note No. 8 be added to read as follows:

       In relation to condition 5 the 'arrangements' concerning the amalgamation of
       lots, the intention is for an application to be lodged with the WAPC.

The Amending Motion was put and CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

REASON: The intention of the condition is to provide some certainty that the process for
amalgamation of lots is underway at the time the application for building permit is lodged
with the City. The process of consideration and approval by the WAPC can then follow
and be completed prior to occupation. The advice note clarifies this process and timeline.

       Emergency Services.

       the intention is for an application to be lodged with the WAPC.

The Primary Motion (as amended) was put and CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

REASON: The proposal is consistent with the Business Development zone and the
General industry category in the draft Structure Plan. The proposal does not undermine
other development or prejudice the implementation of the overall structure plan. All
adjacent lots have adequate access and services. The parking demand analysis supports
limited parking provision for a low intensity employment development. The landscape will
create amenity and screening and is of good quality. Minor variations to setbacks do not
impact adjoining development or the streetscape. The design is otherwise generally
compliant with site requirements. The proposal will support the region’s economic
development in a strategic location and was therefore unanimously supported.
