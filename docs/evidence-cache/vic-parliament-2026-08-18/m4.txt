Subject Site and Surrounds

Site Description
6. The subject site is located at 127 Todd Road, Port Melbourne, within the Fishermans Bend Employment Precinct,
   approximately 4 kilometres southwest of the Melbourne CBD. It is identified for significant employment-generating
   uses and large-format development under the Port Phillip Planning Scheme and the Fishermans Bend Framework.

7. The site is formally described as CP172440L and is held in fee simple by NEXTDC Holdings No. 3 Pty Ltd.

8. The site comprises a large, irregularly shaped parcel of 58,178 m² (5.82 hectares), with substantial frontages to Todd
   Road (approx. 250m), Wharf Road (approx. 425m), and Westgate Park (approx. 396m).

9. The site is generally flat, with no significant changes in level across the landholding, consistent with its former
   industrial use.

10. The site is currently vacant, with the previous building (the historic Westgate Park Print Plant) demolished in 2024.
    The site is predominantly occupied by the former building’s concrete slab and scattered vegetation. There are 125
    individual trees on site, as detailed in the Arboricultural Impact Assessment submitted by the applicant.

11. Vehicle access is provided via a crossover to Todd Road at the southeastern corner. The site is bounded by security
    chain mesh fencing, generally set back 3 metres from the boundary.

12. The site forms part of the visual and ecological interface with Westgate Park and is highly visible from surrounding
    road corridors and parkland.

13. The existing train line and proposed tram line along Wharf Road prevent vehicle crossovers to this boundary, limiting
    vehicular access to Todd Road. The site’s southern boundary interfaces directly with Westgate Park, including
    maintenance access paths and a maintenance shed.

14. The site is well serviced by existing utility infrastructure associated with its former industrial use, including power,
    water, sewer and telecommunications connections.

Site Surrounds
15. Development surrounding the site includes:

    •   To the north of the site is the intersection of Todd Road and Wharf Road, comprising a multi-lane roundabout.
        North of Todd Road are large lots occupied by multi-storey offices and warehouses, including 130–188 Todd
        Road, which is used for advanced manufacturing and aerospace engineering.

    •   To the east, the site abuts the landscaped verge of Todd Road. Across Todd Road is 130–188 Todd Road, a
        substantial lot with several large warehouses. Further east, the area remains industrial in character, with large-
        scale commercial and industrial facilities within the Fishermans Bend Employment Precinct.

    •   To the south of the site is Westgate Park, a regionally significant public open space of approximately 40
        hectares, comprising wetlands, sealed and unsealed pathways, and diverse flora and fauna. The site directly
        abuts a walkway through the northern portion of Westgate Park, which is primarily used for park maintenance.

    •   To the west, the site abuts a landscaped strip within the Wharf Road reserve, which also contains unused rail
        line. Wharf Road is a north–south arterial road comprising a single trafficable lane in each direction and does
        not provide on-street parking. Land further west is characterised by large-scale industrial and commercial uses,
        including the Boeing Aerostructures Australia and Leonardo facilities, both of which are significant employers
        within the precinct.

Strategic Direction and Land Use
Plan for Victoria
23. Plan for Victoria provides a long-term, integrated strategy for the sustainable growth of Victoria, focusing on
    liveability, economic development, and the delivery of essential infrastructure. The plan identifies the importance of
    supporting employment and innovation precincts, particularly those that drive technological advancement and digital
    transformation. The proposed data centre at 127 Todd Road, Port Melbourne, is directly aligned with these
    objectives, as it will deliver critical digital infrastructure, support the growth of advanced manufacturing and
    technology sectors, and contribute to Victoria’s competitiveness in the global economy.

24. The site’s location within the Fishermans Bend Employment Precinct is consistent with Plan for Victoria’s vision for
    state-significant employment clusters. These clusters are intended to attract investment, foster innovation, and create
    high-value jobs. The plan also highlights the need for resilient, future-proofed infrastructure to support economic
    growth and population expansion. The data centre will play a key role in meeting the increasing demand for digital
    services, supporting job creation, and strengthening Victoria’s position as a leader in technology, research, and
    innovation.

Fishermans Bend Vision and Framework
25. The Fishermans Bend Vision (2016) and Fishermans Bend Framework (2018) set out a comprehensive plan for
    transforming Fishermans Bend into a world-class centre for advanced manufacturing, engineering, research, and
    innovation. The Framework designates the Employment Precinct as a National Employment and Innovation Cluster
    (NEIC), targeting the creation of 40,000 jobs by 2050 and supporting globally competitive research and technology
    sectors. The Framework also emphasises the importance of delivering “catalyst” infrastructure projects such as data
    centres to underpin the precinct’s transformation and attract further investment.

Building Height and Massing

50. The proposed building attains a maximum height of 40.5 metres to the upper roof level and 47.5 metres to the top of
    roof plant (see Elevations, Sheet DA13). The building is arranged over a ground floor and five upper levels, with the
    uppermost plant and equipment zones recessed from the primary façades to reduce perceived bulk when viewed
    from the public realm. The overall Gross Floor Area (GFA) is 94,987 m², as confirmed on the Ground Floor Plan and
    Area Schedule (Sheet DA06). The height, scale and intensity of development are commensurate with the site’s
    location within the Fishermans Bend Employment Precinct, which is identified for substantial employment-generating
    and infrastructure-intensive development.

51. The massing is broken into three north–south oriented pods, with data halls occupying the central and southern
    wings, and commercial/office components and the Innovation Hub (Technology Centre of Excellence) located at the
    prominent Todd Road/Wharf Road intersection. This approach reduces the apparent scale of the development and
    avoids the presentation of a single, monolithic built form, consistent with Clause 15.01-1S, which seeks urban
    environments that respond to their context and enhance visual amenity.

52. The articulation of the building into discrete pods also responds to Clause 15.01-2S, which encourages built form
    outcomes that achieve appropriate scale, form and massing, particularly for large developments. The north–south
    orientation improves legibility, creates visual breaks along the street frontage and allows the more publicly accessible
    and active uses to address the key intersection, reinforcing urban structure and wayfinding within the employment
    precinct.

53. Locating the Innovation Hub and office components at the Todd Road/Wharf Road corner supports the objectives of
    Clause 11.01-1S and Clause 17.01-1S by promoting knowledge-based employment, innovation and economic
    productivity in a strategically significant employment area. This interface provides a more articulated and engaging
