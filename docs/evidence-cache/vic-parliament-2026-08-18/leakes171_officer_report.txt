Proposed

5. A separate application to subdivide the subject land has been lodged with the Wyndham Council on 21 February
   2024 for approval (Permit Reference Number WYP14490/24; Certification Reference Number WYS6209/24).

6. The proposed Plan of Subdivision consolidates the land into one lot and proposes to deliver the required roads in

   PSP. A loop road (identified as R2) connecting Balrook Way and Jarklan Street on the eastern site boundary is

Proposal Summary
8. Specific details of the application include:

        •   The use and development of the site for a Utility Installation (Data Centre) with ancillary offices, the display
            of directional signage and car parking. Ancillary works to support the development include a substation to
            support the use and loading docks.

        •   The proposal includes two buildings (Building A and B) which will operate independent of each other with the
            office buildings positioned prominent of the warehouse buildings, with both warehouses featuring an external
            plant area to service the use.

        •   Each data centre will operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week with approximately 60 staff members and 13
            off-peak skeleton staff to maintain security and operations.

        •   Each building site will contain an office space, fire pump, diesel generators, fuel depot, and a catcher room,
            MV room and generator yard to provide a backup source of power. A designated waste storage area will be
            provided the rear of the office buildings.

        •   The data centres will be serviced by a proposed substation to supply power to the buildings and built to the
            Leakes Road front boundary. Backup power will be provided by diesel electrical generators located within
            the external plant area.

        •   Each building will be accessible by internal two-way access roads and serviced by attendant car parks (for
            staff and visitors), bike spaces and loading facilities.

        •   Building A will be located in the northern half of the site, oriented towards Leakes Road. Separate 12m wide
            entry and exit points will service the site and accessed via Leakes Road (westbound approach) through a
            proposed service road. Building B is proposed in the southern portion of the site and oriented towards the
            southern boundary where vehicular access is proposed via Pallarang Way.

        •   The buildings will feature a range of external materials including colorbond metal panels finished in grey and
            cream along the top element of the buildings with concrete wall panels finished in a range of grey and cream

Site Description
10. The site is located on the southern side of Leakes Road, between Palmers Road to the West and Amherst Drive to
    the East. Along the eastern boundary the site has connections to Balrook Way, Jarklan Street and Pallarang Way.

11. The existing site is vacant, generally flat and clear of any vegetation. No formal vehicle access is currently provided
    to the site from Leakes Road. Upon completion of the proposed development, access to the site will be via a new
    service road (westbound approach) along Leakes Road and via Pallarang Way running along the south boundary.

12. The site has a rectangular shape and measures 217.14m with a depth of 559.13m, with a total size of 12.15
    hectares.

13. The site is formally described as comprising the following land parcels:

         •   Lot 3 on Plan of Subdivision 133719 Volume 09395 Folio 748

14. There are no easements, restrictions or reserves on the site. A notice Section 45 Melbourne Strategic Assessment
    (Environmental Mitigation Levy) 2020 AT390578W 01/07/2020 is registered on Title. The payable MSA Levy will be
    confirmed with the Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA) and will be paid prior to the
    issue of a building permit.

15. The subject site is located within the Truganina Employment Precinct Structure Plan (PSP) and identified as ‘General
    Industrial A’ land under the PSP.

Site Surrounds
16. The surrounding development consists mainly of light industrial uses such as large warehouse and distribution
    centres, truck rentals and truck repair centres.

17. Development surrounding the site can be described as follows:

     •   To the north of the site is Leakes Road which is a four-lane wide arterial road with a pedestrian walkway and
         bike path running adjacent. Land across from Leakes Road accommodates a substation and an Amart Furniture
         distribution centre.

     •   To the south and west of the site, land is vacant.

     •   To the east of the site, land accommodates a two-storey warehouse development, an end-to-end ecommerce
         logistics business and a truck yard.

Applicable Zones
22. A planning permit is required to use land for utility installation (Data Centre) and to construct a building or construct
    or carry out works in accordance with Clause 37.07-9 and 37.07-11 of the UGZ2 and Clause 33.02-1 and Clause
    33.02-4 of the INZ2, respectively.

23. The purpose of the UGZ2 is:

     •   To implement the Municipal Planning Strategy and the Planning Policy Framework.

     •   To manage the transition of non-urban land into urban land in accordance with a precinct structure plan.

     •   To provide for a range of uses and the development of land generally in accordance with a precinct structure
         plan.

     •   To contain urban use and development to areas identified for urban development in a precinct structure plan.

     •   To provide for the continued non-urban use of the land until urban development in accordance with a precinct
         structure plan occurs.

     •   To ensure that, before a precinct structure plan is applied, the use and development of land does not prejudice
         the future urban use and development of the land.

24. The purpose of the INZ2 is:

     •   To implement the Municipal Planning Strategy and the Planning Policy Framework.

     •   To provide for manufacturing industry, the storage and distribution of goods and associated facilities in a
         manner which does not affect the safety and amenity of local communities.

     •   To promote manufacturing industries and storage facilities that require a substantial threshold distance within
         the core of the zone.

     •   To keep the core of the zone free of uses which are suitable for location elsewhere so as to be available for
         manufacturing industries and storage facilities that require a substantial threshold distance as the need for these
         arises.

Provisions that Require, Enable or Exempt a permit
29. Clause 52.05 sets out the requirements for signage. Pursuant to Clause 52.05-12, a permit is required for the display
    of more than one directional sign per premises.

30. Clause 52.06 sets out the requirements of car parking provision and design. Clause 52.06-5 (Table 1 - Car parking
    requirement) does not include the land use ‘data centre’, as such pursuant to Clause 52.06-6, car parking spaces
    must be provided to the satisfaction of the responsible authority.

General Requirements and Performance Standards
31. Clause 53.10 applies to uses with adverse amenity potential. The Proposal includes the storage of diesel in
    quantities of approximately 250kL, which is less than the threshold limit set out in the Table to Clause 53.10-2 (2000
    tonnes). Accordingly, there is no referral required under Clause 53.10-1 to the Environment Protection Authority.

32. Clause 53.18 applies to the buildings and works of this application. The clause includes standards and objectives
    relating to stormwater management. The proposed site is located within Melbourne Water’s Doherty’s Drain
    Development Services Scheme (DSS) as detailed in the Stormwater Management Plan. The DSS ensures planning
    for urban development is conducted on a catchment wide basis and meets appropriate standards for flood protection
    and environmental performance, including protection and enhancement of water and biodiversity values. The
    Applicant will continue to engage with Melbourne Water and Wyndham Council throughout the design and
    development phase (separate to this planning application) and prior to lodgement of any subdivision plans to ensure
    all stormwater management is appropriate and avoids potential impacts.

33. Clause 53.22 Significant Economic Development seeks to:

     •   To prioritise and facilitate the planning, assessment and delivery of projects that will make a significant
         contribution to Victoria’s economy and provide substantial public benefit, including jobs for Victorians.

     •   To provide for the efficient and effective use of land and facilitate use and development with high quality urban
         design, architecture and landscape architecture.

34. The proposal is submitted under Category 1, having met the relevant conditions and eligibility criteria of this Clause.

Other Strategic Considerations
Structure Plan
35. The subject site is located within the Truganina Employment PSP, 19 kilometres west of central Melbourne along the
    West Gate Transport Corridor within the City of Wyndham. The precinct is part of the Western Industrial Node – one
    of Melbourne’s three major industrial nodes – which extends from Yarraville to Derrimut to form one of Australia’s
    largest, contiguous industrial areas.

36. The vision for the Truganina Employment PSP area is:

    “To provide diverse employment opportunities for Wyndham and the western region of Melbourne by establishing a
    thriving industrial employment precinct that extends west Western Industrial Node and provides for a wide range of
    businesses and jobs. The precinct will comprise quality industrial park developments with a logistics bias and have
    strong links to industrial activity centres and future higher order services.”

37. The subject site has been categorised as ‘General Industrial A’ land which dictates that the predominant land use will
    accommodate industrial and manufacturing uses. Heavy and general industry, manufacturing, logistics and other
    related activities may be located in this area.

Strategic Direction and Land Use
48. The Planning Policy Framework encourage appropriate land use and development which enhances the built
    environment, supports economic growth, meets the community expectations on retail and commercial provision,
    delivers diversity in housing supply to meet existing and future needs, and integrates transport and infrastructure
    planning.

49. The relevant MPS and PPF policies have been considered in assessing the application.

50. The proposed land use is consistent with the strategic direction of the following policies:

         •   In accordance with Clause 11.03-2S Growth Areas, the proposal supports the ongoing growth of the
             Truganina Employment Precinct by creating jobs and attracting similar ‘new technology’ projects in the area.

         •   In accordance with Clause 13 Environmental Risks and Amenity, the proposal will not have any detrimental
             impacts to the amenity of the surrounding area, as there are no sensitive uses adjoining or nearby in the
             area. Utilising the land for a data centre is consistent with the intended use of the land under the relevant
             PSP which designates the area for heavy and general industry, manufacturing and logistics.

         •   The proposal is consistent with the provisions of Clause 15 Built Environment and Heritage as it provides a
             modern and architecturally designed development which will enhance the amenity of the area. The main
             considerations around the building layouts and design are security and functionality to protect the
             development from threats that could comprise its confidentiality and availability of business information
             assets or intellectual property. The built form and façade treatment responds to the immediate context of the
             site using a range of contemporary building materials and finishes such as vertical metal ribbing and textured
             concrete panelling.

         •   Native and draught tolerant species are proposed for landscaped areas, as well as trees to the Leakes Road
             and Pallarang Way boundaries.

         •   In accordance with Clause 17.01-1 Employment supports the objectives of the West Growth Area Corridor
             Plan and PSP which aims to provide diversified employment opportunities close to where people live. The
             proposal provides industrial employment opportunities for nearby residential areas west of Palmers Road.
