Water Sector Working Group
on Large Non-Residential
Water Users
Terms of Reference
1. Purpose

      The Working Group will coordinate the Melbourne metropolitan water sector’s
      approach to managing large non-residential water users, including data centres,
      ensuring consistency, transparency, and alignment with Victorian Government policy
      objectives. The group will:

  •       Provide a forum for collaboration across water corporations and DEECA under the
          Managing Director (MD) Accord.

  •       Support the development of sector-wide policy, process, and guidance materials.

  •       Identify and manage risks to water security, customer equity, and environmental
          sustainability.

  •       Enable a proactive and coordinated response to emerging high-demand connection
          requests.

2. Scope

The Working Group will:

      •    Develop and maintain a sector-wide incident action plan for large non-residential
           water users.

      •    Advise on application assessment frameworks, referral processes, and eligibility
           criteria.

      •    Coordinate scenario modelling of future demand and infrastructure requirements.

  •     Creating a public-facing sector document outlining policies and requirements for
        large users.

  •     Support consistent implementation of state government policy principles across
        corporations.

  •     Consider long-term system responses to support water efficiency and use of non-
        potable sources.

3. Governance

      The Working Group operates under the oversight of the MD Accord governance structure
      (via the GMSF). DEECA and VicWater will participate as standing members, ensuring
      alignment with government priorities and providing policy input. The Working Group will
      report into established MD Accord cadences with written updates as required.

4. Membership

  •     Representatives (Team Leader / Manager level) from each metropolitan water
        corporation.

  •     Representatives from relevant water corporations and VicWater.

  •     DEECA (Water and Catchments Group) representative.

  •     Other stakeholders (e.g. DJSIR) may be invited as required.

5. Roles and Responsibilities

  •     Working Group Lead: Facilitate meetings, set agenda, and ensure actions are tracked
        across Program.

  •     Members: Provide input, share insights, and represent their organisations’
        perspectives.

  •     Secretariat (host or Accord secretariat): Prepare agendas, record minutes, logistics.

6. Meetings

  •     Frequency: Monthly, or more frequently as required during active incidents (e.g.
        multiple concurrent applications or critical activities).

  •     Mode: Hybrid (in-person and online).

  •     Quorum: At least three corporations.

7. Deliverables

  •   Sector-wide public-facing document capturing key policies, processes, and
      obligations for large non-residential users.

  •   Quarterly reports to MD Accord Executive Group summarising applications, risks,
      and sector response.

  •   Annual review of infrastructure readiness and policy alignment.

  •   Recommendations to water corporations and DEECA on long-term system strategies
      for high-demand customers.

8. Review

  •   These Terms of Reference will be reviewed at least quarterly or in the event of
      significant change by the Working Group and endorsed by the MD Accord Executive
      Group.
