CENTRE
P1 MALAGA EXPANSION

   DAP Presentation – 8 May 2024
NEXTDC INTRODUCTION
PROJECT BACKGROUND
Existing NEXTDC P1 Data Centre approved by JDAP in 2011.
The approved building comprised a three storey, 8,166sq.m building.
21% (1,736sq.m) of the site was provided as landscaping.
Demand for Data Centres has resulted in need for expansion.
P1 EXPANSION – PROPOSED DEVELOPMENT
•   Six storey development with a total floor area of approx. 6,670 sq.m
•   Data halls from Floor 1 – 3, chillers, pumps and generators on Floor 4 & 5.
•   660sq.m of office space and four breakout space.
•   38 car parking bays
•   Approx 10.4% of site area as landscaping
PLOT RATIO
• Site is zoned ‘General Industrial’, with a plot ratio control of 0.6:1. This control is intended
  to manage ‘site cover’ in an industrial area.
• Plot ratio of 2.4:1 is proposed. This has been supported by both the DRP and City, due to
  the buildings response to context and site design.
• Importantly the design provides all essential ground level design expectations – e.g.
  landscape quality, setbacks, refuse and circulation areas.
CAR PARKING
• Data Centre is classified as a ‘Warehouse’. The floor area based parking standard for a
  ‘Warehouse’ results in the on-paper parking shortfall of 332 bays.
• In practice, floor area is not a suitable control for parking for a Data Centre, and this
  quantity of bays is not required for the NEXTDC operation.
• A typical operation is not expected to require more then 38 bays. A parking management
  plan will also be provided to manage occasional events where additional visitors or staff
  are required.

 CONDITION 4 & 5 – MINOR CHANGE
Existing Conditions 4 & 5
4. The Data Centre (Warehouse) is limited to a maximum number of 38 people (staff and customers) at
any given time and up to 90 employees in emergency periods.
5. Prior to occupation or use of the development, a parking management plan is to be submitted to the
City for approval detailing how the 90 employees during emergency periods will be managed.


Proposed New Condition

The Data Centre (Warehouse) is limited to a maximum number of 38 people (staff and
customers) on site at any one time. Additional employees and customers may be
permitted during emergency periods and events, subject to a parking management plan
being approved by the City of Swan prior to occupation of the development.

Rationale: to simplify the condition and provide more flexibility to NEXTDC’s operations.
Revised condition wording has been supported by the City.
